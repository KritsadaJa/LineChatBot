# Solar System Components Comparison

> **Short Answer**: NextE uses Tier-1 monocrystalline solar panels and high-efficiency inverters (string or micro) based on your roof characteristics and budget.

---

## 💬 Customer Explanation

### What equipment does NextE use?

**Solar Panels**: We use **monocrystalline** panels (black, high efficiency) which perform better in hot weather and low-light conditions compared to older polycrystalline (blue) panels.

**Inverters**: The "brain" of the system.
- **String Inverters**: Cost-effective, ideal for roofs without shading.
- **Micro-inverters/Optimizers**: Panel-level monitoring, ideal for shaded or complex roof shapes.

**Racking System**: Aluminum structures designed specifically for your roof type (tile, metal sheet, corrugated) to guarantee waterproofing and wind resistance.

**NextE Standards**: We only use **financially stable Tier-1 manufacturers** with proven track records and strong warranties.

### Panel Degradation

- **Year 1**: Loss ≤ 2%
- **Years 2–25**: Loss ≤ 0.55% per year
- **Year 30**: Still produces ≥ 87% of rated power

---

## 💰 Pricing and Benefits

### Impact on Costs

**Micro-inverters**: Increase equipment costs by approximately 10–15% but improve energy harvest in shaded conditions.

**Tier-1 Panels**: May have a slightly higher price than Tier-2 but offer warranty security and better resale value.

**Commercial Scale (C&I)**: Large string inverters significantly reduce cost-per-watt for systems >100kW.

---

## 🔄 Workflow

### Equipment Selection Logic

1. **Roof Assessment**: If shading is >5%, we recommend panel-level equipment (Micro/Optimizers).
2. **Aesthetic Preference**: All-black panels are available for visual consistency on residential homes.
3. **Budget Alignment**: We offer Good/Better/Best options during the quoting stage.
4. **Procurement**: Equipment is reserved upon contract signing to lock in pricing.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What happens if the inverter breaks?**  
**A**: The inverter is the component most likely to need replacement (typically after 10–15 years). NextE's warranty covers labor for installation-related issues.

**Q: Do you use cheap panels?**  
**A**: No. NextE only uses Tier-1 manufacturers rated for financial stability by BloombergNEF.

**Q: Can I choose my own equipment?**  
**A**: We recommend our pre-designed packages to maintain warranty integrity, but specific brands can be discussed during the design phase.
