## 1. Huawei FusionSolar

### Product Overview
Huawei offers a comprehensive Smart PV Solution for residential homes, featuring the latest technology for asymmetric operation and backup power.

### Inverter Models

#### **SUN2000-MAP0 Series** (Newest Model)
- **Size**: 5-12 kW (3-Phase)
- **Key Features**: 
  - Asymmetric Output - Independent power control for each phase.
  - Supports 100% Unbalanced Load.
  - Supports 200% Short-time Overload.
  - ESS awake by grid.
  - Supports up to 3 inverters (MAP0).

#### **SUN2000-M1/MB0 Series**
- **M1**: 3-10 kW (1-Phase/3-Phase)
- **MB0**: 15-20 kW (3-Phase)
- Supports AFCI, RSD, and Optimizers.

#### **SUN2000-L1/LC0 Series**
- **L1**: 3-5 kW (1-Phase)
- **LC0**: 10 kW (1-Phase)
- Built-in WiFi module.

### Energy Storage System (ESS)

**LUNA2000 Series**:
- **S0**: 5/10/15 kWh (400V)
- **S1**: 7/14/21 kWh (48V)
- **Connectivity**:
  - M1/MAP0: Up to 2 ESS units.
  - MB0: Up to 4 ESS units.
  - L1/LC0: Up to 2 ESS units.

### Backup Systems

**SmartGuard-63A**:
- Supports Whole Home Backup for homes ≤63A.
- Partial Home Backup for homes >63A.
- Switchover time: 20ms (MAP0), 100ms (M1/MB0).
- Supports Three-phase backup (MAP0 only).
- Supports DG (Diesel Generator) + ATS.

- SUN2000: 10 Years*
- SUN5000: 15 Years*
- LUNA Battery: 10 Years
*\*Terms vary by region.*
