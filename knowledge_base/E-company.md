## ❓ NextE FAQ

**Q: How many years until the solar system pays for itself?** **A**: On average 3-5 years, depending on the system size and daytime electricity consumption.

**Q: Why is Sunpower more expensive than Jinko?** **A**: Sunpower offers a 30/30 year warranty (product/performance) and has a lower degradation rate.

**Q: Does NextE handle the permit process?** **A**: Yes, we provide a "Free" end-to-end service including MEA/PEA, ERC notification, and Factory License (R.N.4) exemptions.

**Q: How long is the installation warranty?** **A**: 
- Installation and Structure: 5 years.
- Equipment: Per manufacturer (Panels 12-30 years, Inverters 10-25 years).
