# Solar Rooftop Installation Process

## ⏳ Timeline Summary
**Approximate Duration**: 2.5-4.5 months (10-18 weeks)

| Step | Duration | Details |
|---------|----------|------------|
| **1. Survey & Design** | 2-3 Days | Engineers site visit and system sizing. |
| **2. Installation** | 2-3 Days | Physical install of panels and inverter. |
| **3. Permits** | 1-2 Months | MEA/PEA, ERC, and Factory License exemption. |
| **4. Grid Connection** | 1-2 Months | Authority inspection and meter change. |

---

## 📋 Process Details

### 1️⃣ Survey & Design (2-3 Days)
- Roof structure inspection and Shading Analysis.
- Outcome: Electrical Single Line Diagram and finalized quote.

### 2️⃣ Installation (2-3 Days)
- Mounting structure, panels, inverter, and ground system.
- Safety Standards: Compliance with IEC, Surge Protection, and Rapid Shutdown.

### 3️⃣ Government Permitting (1-2 Months)
- **MEA/PEA**: Grid connection application.
- **ERC (กกพ.)**: Notification of energy business operation (Ak.1).
- **Factory Dept**: R.N.4 exemption (Solar roofs do not require factory licenses).

### 4️⃣ Inspection & Synchronization (1-2 Months)
- Utility safety inspection and installation of the bidirectional meter.

---

## 🎯 Legal Requirements
- **Panels**: Must meet TIS 2725-2559 or IEC 61215/61730.
- **Inverters**: Must meet TIS 3040-2558 or IEC 62109, with Anti-Islanding and Rapid Shutdown.
- **Structure**: Aluminum or Stainless Steel, wind resistant ≥ 130 km/h.
