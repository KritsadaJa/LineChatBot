# Solar PV Fundamentals

> **Short Answer**: Solar panels convert sunlight into Direct Current (DC) electricity. An inverter then changes it to Alternating Current (AC) for use in buildings, reducing electricity bills.

---

## 💬 Customer Explanation

### How Solar Works (Simplified)

1. **Photovoltaic Effect**: Sunlight hits the solar cells, causing electrons to move and create DC electricity.
2. **Inversion**: The inverter converts DC to AC electricity, which home appliances and the grid use.
3. **Usage**: Solar power is used by the building first. Any excess can be stored in batteries (selling back to the grid is currently limited/restricted in some contexts).
4. **Nighttime**: At night or on cloudy days, the system automatically draws power from the grid as usual.

### Key Units of Measurement

| Unit | Meaning | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **kW (Kilowatt)** | System capacity/power | A 6kW system |
| **kWh (Kilowatt-hour)** | Energy produced/used over time | Using a 1kW heater for 1 hour = 1kWh |
| **Peak Sun Hours** | Hours of max intensity sunlight | Thailand: 4-5 hours/day |

---

## ⚙️ Technical Details

### Factors Affecting Efficiency

- **Irradiance**: Measured in kWh/m²/day. Thailand averages 4-5 Peak Sun Hours.
- **Temperature Coefficient**: Output drops slightly as panels get hot (~ -0.3% to -0.4% per °C above 25°C).
- **Degradation**: Panels lose efficiency over time (~ 0.4-0.55% per year).
- **Shading**: Even 10% shading can significantly drop output without optimizers or microinverters.
- **Orientation**: South-facing at a 10-15 degree tilt provides the highest yield in Thailand.

---

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Does it work on cloudy days?** **A**: Yes, but output drops to 10–25% of normal depending on cloud density.

**Q: Does solar work during a power outage?** **A**: No. Without a battery, grid-tied systems shut down automatically for safety (Anti-islanding).

**Q: How long do panels last?** **A**: Most manufacturers warrant 25–30 years. They usually continue producing power at a reduced efficiency (≥87% at year 30) after that.
